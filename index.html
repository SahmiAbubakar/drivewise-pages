Straight answers

Before you trust DriveWise with a tough decision.

Can DriveWise tell me if a quote is a scam?

No app can know a shop’s intent. DriveWise can show when a supported quote appears high against a broad reference range, explain what the repair means, and suggest questions or a second opinion before you approve non-emergency work.

Does DriveWise replace a mechanic?

No. DriveWise provides general educational guidance based on the details you enter. It cannot inspect or diagnose your vehicle and does not replace a certified mechanic.

What if my car feels unsafe?

Do not drive it. Ask the shop whether the vehicle should be towed or inspected immediately. Safety should always come before comparing prices.

Does my information leave my phone?

Not in this version. DriveWise stores the quotes and vehicle details you enter locally on your device and does not require an account.

DriveWise

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

DriveWise stores the quote and vehicle details you enter on your device, including vehicle year, make, model, mileage, shop name, quote details, symptoms, warning-light status, drivability, saved analysis, and app preferences.

Accounts

DriveWise does not require an account.

Data Storage

DriveWise does not use a backend server in this version. Your saved data remains on your device until you delete individual quotes or vehicles, delete all local data in Settings, or remove the app.

Sharing

DriveWise does not sell or share your personal information with third parties.

Educational Guidance

DriveWise provides educational guidance based on general repair information and user-entered details. It is not a substitute for inspection or advice from a certified mechanic. If your vehicle feels unsafe, do not drive it.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, contact drivewisehelp@yahoo.com.
